Curious about how machines learn, and who they help.

Things I've built

computer vision · health

Skin Lesion Classification under Distribution Shift live

Fine-tuned a Vision Transformer three ways (linear probe, full fine-tune, LoRA) on dermoscopy images, then tested on smartphone photos, where accuracy dropped 25–31 points. I added a shift detector that flags unfamiliar photos before predicting (AUROC 0.95 across 5 seeds, catching 71% of smartphone photos vs 24% for the model's own confidence), and deployed it as a live demo.
